# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Jam-Sw to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless
of experience, identity, or background. Everyone who takes part in our projects
is expected to act with respect and good faith.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behavior that is unacceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **jam.sw.org@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments,
and contributors that violate this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
